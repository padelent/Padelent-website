Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Padelent is a new online padel coaching service, launching soon. This page explains what we do with the details you give us when you sign up for updates on padelent.com.

What we collect

Your email address, whether you signed up as a player or a coach, and the date you signed up.

Why we collect it

To tell you when Padelent opens and, if you are a coach, to send you details about coaching on Padelent. We will not sell your details or share them with anyone for their own marketing.

Our legal basis

Your consent, which you give when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time.

Where it is kept

Your details are stored securely by our database provider, Supabase, which processes them on our behalf.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove you, or for no longer than 24 months after you sign up if we have not launched by then.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your details, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time. Email info@padelent.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
