Padelent is an online marketplace that connects padel players with padel coaches for video lessons. A player sends a coach clips of their game, and the coach replies with personal video feedback.

4. Lessons

Coaches choose which lesson options they offer:

  • Free trial lesson. One per player with each coach, at no cost.
  • Single lesson. One lesson at the price shown on the coach's profile.
  • Monthly plan. A monthly subscription with a set number of lessons a week, at the price shown on the coach's profile. Monthly plans will be available once card payments are live.

After a coach replies, you can keep chatting about the lesson until the coach closes it.

Coaching is advice based on the clips you send. We do not promise any particular result.
