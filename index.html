How it works

Three steps, no court booking.

1

Choose your coach

Browse coach profiles, see what each one specialises in and what they charge, and pick the one that fits your game.

2

Send your video

Prop your phone at the back of the court, film a few points, and upload the clip with a note on what you want fixed.

3

Get your lesson back

Your coach replies with a video breakdown and drills. Ask follow-up questions in the same thread whenever you get stuck.

Why Padelent

A person who watches your game, not a bot that scores it.

Questions

What do I need to film?

A phone. Lean it against the back glass or clip it to the fence, and film a few points or a few repeats of one shot.

How much does a lesson cost?

Each coach sets their own price, shown on their profile before you pay.

Do I need to be a good player?

No. Tell your coach your level when you send your first video and they will pitch the feedback to suit you.
